Chess Hub sound effects
=======================

All five sounds are derived from real recordings released under
Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 1.0, public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is
required; it is given here as a courtesy and for provenance.

Licence verification (2026-10-29): each source page below was fetched with
curl and its licence block read "Creative Commons 0" linking to
creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0. Source audio is each page's
"HQ preview" MP3 (cdn.freesound.org/previews/...), a transcode of the
original upload; the original files need a Freesound login.

Sources
-------
[A] "Chess Pieces Move (Close)" by JJTaynos (Freesound #733927)
    https://freesound.org/people/JJTaynos/sounds/733927/
    Recording of chess pieces being moved on a board.
[B] "chess pieces.wav" by simone_ds (Freesound #366065)
    https://freesound.org/people/simone_ds/sounds/366065/
    Chess pieces put down on a board with varying hardness.
[C] Marimba one-shots by sgossner, from Versilian Studios Chamber
    Orchestra 2: Community Edition (pack "VSCO 2 CE - Percussion - Marimba")
    C5: https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/sounds/373580/
        original file Marimba_hit_Outrigger_C4_loud_01.wav
    G5: https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/sounds/373586/
        original file Marimba_hit_Outrigger_G4_loud_01.wav
    F4: https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/sounds/373583/
        original file Marimba_hit_Outrigger_F3_loud_01.wav
    (pitches measured; the file names are one octave low)

Files
-----
move.mp3     [A], single placement at 40.953 s.
capture.mp3  [A], light placement at 40.953 s (-9 dB) followed 38 ms
             later by the heavier placement at 38.444 s (a double knock).
check.mp3    [B], single bright placement at 18.720 s.
success.mp3  [C] C5, then G5 110 ms later (rising fifth).
fail.mp3     [C] C5, then F4 130 ms later (falling fifth).

Processing (ffmpeg 9.0.2)
-------------------------
- Cut from about 4 ms before the transient; downmixed to mono, 44.1 kHz.
- High-pass at 100 Hz (knocks) / 80 Hz (marimba) to remove room rumble.
- Lengths 0.26-0.30 s (knocks) and 0.80-0.85 s (marimba), with
  0.12 s / 0.40 s linear fade-outs.
- Loudness matched: knocks to -21 dBFS RMS over the first 80 ms, marimba
  cues to -24 dBFS RMS over the first 300 ms, then a fast peak limiter at
  -1.5 dBFS (only check.mp3 is touched). Result: all within about
  1.6 LU of each other (K-weighted), peaks -1.7 to -14.6 dBFS.
- Encoded with LAME, MP3 CBR 96 kbps mono.
