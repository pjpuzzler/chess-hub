# Chess Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Chess Hub is a Reddit app (built on Reddit's Developer Platform) that adds chess puzzles, move
polls, game reviews, Guess the Elo and community games to subreddits that install it. This policy
explains what it stores and why.

## What Chess Hub stores

Everything is stored with Reddit's Developer Platform, separately for each subreddit that installs
the app. Chess Hub only sees your Reddit username and only when you use the app while logged in.

- **Puzzle results:** which puzzles you attempted or solved, your streak, puzzle rating and how
  long a solve took (used for leaderboards and to spot cheating).
- **Votes and guesses:** your move in move polls and community games, and your Guess the Elo
  guesses, so you can vote once and see results.
- **Posts you create:** the position, PGN or caption you submit for a poll, game review or Guess
  my Elo post, and your username as its author.
- **Leaderboards:** your username and scores in that subreddit. Moderators can remove a user from
  a leaderboard.

Chess Hub does not use cookies or trackers, show ads, or sell or share data with anyone.

## Outside requests

When someone pastes a lichess.org game link to create a review or Guess my Elo post, the app's
server requests that game's PGN from lichess.org. Only the game ID is sent. No Reddit data is
sent to Lichess or anywhere else.

The chess engine (Stockfish) runs on your own device. Positions you analyze are not uploaded.

## How long data is kept

- Who voted for what in a move poll or community game is deleted 30 days after the vote closes.
  The totals stay so the results can still be shown.
- Your puzzle stats, streak, puzzle attempts and Elo guesses are deleted after 400 days without
  any activity.
- When a post is deleted, everything Chess Hub stored for that post is deleted.
- When you delete your Reddit account, Chess Hub deletes your username and everything tied to it,
  within 30 days.
- Uninstalling the app from a subreddit removes the app's access to that subreddit's data.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at the project's source repository or send a
message to the developer on Reddit (u/pjpuzzler).
